An affiliate link is a link to a shop, such as Amazon, that includes the linking website's own ID. If a visitor follows it and buys something, the shop pays that website a small commission. Amazon's program is called Amazon Associates, and sites in it must tell visitors they earn from these links, which is why many pages carry an affiliate disclosure.
