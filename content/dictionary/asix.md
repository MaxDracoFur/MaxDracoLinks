ASIX Electronics is a Taiwanese company that designs network chips, best known for the chips inside many {{< term "usb" >}}-to-{{< term "ethernet" >}} adapters. Along with {{< term "realtek" >}}, it supplies most of the chips in these dongles, so the brand on the box often matters less than which chip is inside. Each chip needs its own {{< term "driver" >}} in the operating system, and how well that driver handles the chip decides how reliable the adapter is.
