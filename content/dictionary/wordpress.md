WordPress is free, {{< term "open-source" >}} software for running websites, from personal blogs to online shops. The look of a site comes from a theme and extra features come from plugins, many written by other people. Its themes and plugins leave recognisable names in a page's code, and because it is so widely used, outdated plugins are a frequent way into websites.
