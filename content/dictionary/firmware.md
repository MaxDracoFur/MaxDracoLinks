Firmware is the software built into a piece of hardware, such as a {{< term "router" >}}, a {{< term "network-switch" "network switch" >}} or a {{< term "usb" >}} gadget, that makes it work. It sits closer to the hardware than ordinary apps and is usually changed only by installing a new version from the maker, or sometimes an open replacement. Because it runs underneath everything else, a flaw in firmware is hard to see and stays until someone updates it.
