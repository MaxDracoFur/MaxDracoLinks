Tethering is using a phone as a small {{< term "router" >}}: a laptop or tablet connects to the phone, and the phone passes its traffic over the mobile network. The link to the phone can be {{< term "wi-fi" >}} (a "hotspot"), a {{< term "usb" >}} cable, or Bluetooth. It is a common way to get online when the only other choice is an untrusted public network.
