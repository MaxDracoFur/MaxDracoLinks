Netgear is a US company, founded in 1996, that sells networking equipment for homes and small offices: {{< term "router" "routers" >}}, {{< term "wi-fi" >}} systems and {{< term "network-switch" "switches" >}}. Its cheaper "Plus" switches can be set up with {{< term "vlan" "VLANs" >}} but have far fewer settings than enterprise models. Like any device maker's, its {{< term "firmware" "firmware" >}} needs updating when security flaws are found.
