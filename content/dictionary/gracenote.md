Gracenote is a company that runs a large commercial database for identifying music, films and TV, and is now owned by Nielsen. It began as CDDB, a service that let CD players and {{< term "cd-ripping" "ripping programs" >}} look up an album's artist and track names from the layout of the disc. Apps, car stereos and media players send it a {{< term "disc-id" "disc ID" >}} or a sound sample and get back the names to display.
