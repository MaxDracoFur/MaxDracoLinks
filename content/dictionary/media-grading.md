Second-hand music is priced by condition, and the scale almost everyone uses came out of *Goldmine* magazine's guide for records: Mint, Near Mint, Very Good Plus, Very Good, Good or Good Plus, and Poor or Fair. {{< term "discogs" >}} adopted that scale and its sellers extended it with definitions for CDs and cassettes. Grading is done by eye rather than by playing the item, so a seller is expected to grade conservatively and describe anything the grade doesn't cover. On a CD the disc and its printed parts — booklet, inlay, digipak — are graded separately; a standard jewel case is counted as replaceable and isn't graded at all.
