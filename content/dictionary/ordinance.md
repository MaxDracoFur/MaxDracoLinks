An ordinance is a law made by a city or town for the area it governs: how businesses are licensed, what can be built where, what counts as a nuisance. A city's ordinances collected together are its **municipal code**, usually published online and organised by title, chapter and section, so a rule can be cited exactly, like "4-156-300(a)". Breaking one is usually a fine rather than a criminal charge, and the fine can fall on more than one person at a time.
