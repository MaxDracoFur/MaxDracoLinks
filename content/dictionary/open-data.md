Open data is information published so that anyone can use it: downloadable in bulk, free, and licensed so you may reuse and republish it. Cities use it for registers that were always public on paper but awkward to get at, such as who holds which business licence, where building permits were issued, or which streets were resurfaced. A table published this way is usually searchable in a browser and also readable by a program, so you can check one address by hand or thousands at once. What it won't tell you is anything the register doesn't record.
