"Road warrior" is the name for a {{< term "vpn" >}} setup where a travelling device, like a laptop or phone, connects back to a fixed home or office network from wherever it is: a hotel, a café, a phone hotspot. The fixed side waits for connections; the travelling side starts them. Because the travelling device can be anywhere, its owner has to decide in advance what happens when a hostile network blocks the connection.
