MusicBrainz is an open music encyclopedia, edited by volunteers and run by the non-profit MetaBrainz Foundation. It records artists, albums and individual pressings, and {{< term "cd-ripping" "CD-ripping" >}} programs look discs up in it by their {{< term "disc-id" "disc ID" >}} to fill in track names automatically. Its data is free to download and reuse, and it has a public {{< term "api" >}} for programs.
