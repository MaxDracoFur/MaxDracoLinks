Discogs is a catalogue of music releases built by its own members, who add each pressing of each album, and a marketplace sitting on top of it where they sell second-hand copies to each other. Because every listing hangs off a catalogued release, a seller lists the exact pressing rather than just the album, and buyers can search for one. Members also mark releases they have and releases they want, which is the closest thing the site has to a public read on demand.
