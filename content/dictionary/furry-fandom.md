The furry fandom is a worldwide community of fans of anthropomorphic animal characters: animals given human traits, such as walking, talking or wearing clothes. Many fans, called furries, create an animal character of their own to represent themselves, and the community is known for its art, stories and online groups. Some fans wear full animal costumes called fursuits, and dozens of conventions are held around the world each year.
