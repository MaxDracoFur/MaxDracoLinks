Linux is an {{< term "open-source" >}} operating system {{< term "kernel" >}}, the core program that runs the hardware and shares it between other programs. On its own it is just the kernel; projects package it with tools and apps into complete systems called {{< term "linux-distribution" "distributions" >}}. It runs most of the world's servers and cloud machines, Android phones, {{< term "router" "routers" >}} and many other small devices.
