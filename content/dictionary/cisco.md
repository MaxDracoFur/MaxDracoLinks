Cisco Systems is a large US company best known for the {{< term "network-switch" "switches" >}} and {{< term "router" "routers" >}} that run many company and internet-provider networks. Its equipment has many of its own features and protocols, and other vendors often copy or support them. Because Cisco gear is so widespread, flaws in it are a frequent target for attackers.
