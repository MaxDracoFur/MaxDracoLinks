A homelab is a small collection of computers, storage and networking equipment that a person runs at home, usually to learn, try ideas out and {{< term "self-hosting" "host their own services" >}} instead of relying on outside companies. It can be anything from one old laptop to a rack of servers. Because a homelab often holds real data and can be reached from the internet, it needs the same care as a business network: updates, backups and a {{< term "firewall" >}}.
