Realtek Semiconductor is a Taiwanese company that designs chips. Its {{< term "ethernet" >}}, {{< term "wi-fi" >}} and audio chips are cheap and everywhere: in motherboards, laptops, {{< term "router" "routers" >}} and {{< term "usb" >}} network adapters. How well one of those adapters works often depends less on the chip than on the {{< term "driver" >}} the operating system ships for it.
