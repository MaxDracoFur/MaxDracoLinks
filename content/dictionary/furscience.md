Furscience is the public face of the International Anthropomorphic Research Project (IARP), a team of academic scientists who have surveyed and studied the {{< term "furry-fandom" >}} since 2011. It publishes its findings in research papers and in plain-language summaries for fans, parents, journalists and clinicians, with the aim of replacing myths about furries with evidence.
