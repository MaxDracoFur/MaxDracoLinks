AdSense is {{< term "google" >}}'s ad network for website owners. The owner adds a short piece of code to their pages, Google fills the space with ads, and the owner is paid a share of what advertisers spend. Each account has a publisher ID that starts with ca-pub- followed by sixteen digits, and it sits in the public code of every page that shows the ads, so the same ID on two sites means the same account is being paid.
