SEO (search engine optimisation) is the work of making a website show up higher in search results, for example on {{< term "google" >}}. Some of it is simply making pages clear and useful; some of it is a race to game the ranking, such as buying old websites that search engines already trust. Search engines publish rules against the tricks, and pages that break them can be pushed down or removed.
