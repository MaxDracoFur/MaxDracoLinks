I'm a furry hacker, in Chicago, trying to be a self-sufficient business owner against the odds. Do or die.

I want to work hard and say I earned it, even when the easy way was better paid.

## What I'm doing here

When I was a kid I wrote a choose-your-own-adventure game on a calculator. It gave me freedom to write how the world can and should work when it wasn't. I've been doing a version of that ever since.

So: I build the thing that works, and then I don't keep it to myself. I'd rather spread the knowledge and let people live freer, against the common enemy, entropy.

Fuck exclusion. I've helped people get in the door before and I'll do it again.

## What's on this site

- **[Furcago](https://furcago.com/)** — the furries of Chicago: the community's site and its directory of local chats.
- **[Max Draco CDs](https://cds.maxdraco.com/)** — sell me your CDs, or browse the store. Graded and shipped from Chicago.
- **[Security research](https://hack.maxdraco.com/)** — hardware, network and badge hacking, written up.
- **[The blog](/blog/)** — what I find and check.

## What I care about

I give a crap about the quality of this world, regardless of who was born rich. I want people to do well, have freedom, and work in a functional place.
