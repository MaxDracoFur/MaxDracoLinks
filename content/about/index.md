I'm Max Draco. I buy and sell used CDs in Chicago, I run Furcago, the city's furry meetup group, and I write up the security work I do at home.

This site is the front door to all three, and the blog is where I put what I find along the way.

- **[Max Draco CDs](https://cds.maxdraco.com/)** — sell me your CDs, or browse the store. Graded and shipped from Chicago.
- **[Furcago](https://furcago.com/)** — the furries of Chicago, and a directory of local chats.
- **[Security research](https://hack.maxdraco.com/)** — writeups.
- **[Blog](/blog/)** — what I find and check.

If you have CDs to sell, or you want to ask about any of it, email me at [admin@maxdraco.com](mailto:admin@maxdraco.com).
