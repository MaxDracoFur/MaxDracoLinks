{{/* Watermark: resize an image resource to width .w and stamp "© maxdraco.com" bottom-right in the brand colors: lime text (--lime) with a solid purple outline (Max 2026-11-12: "make it purple/green"). images.Text has no bold or stroke option, so: the lime copy is stamped on a small $b x $b grid (faux bold), and the purple copy around it at $o past each edge (outline).
